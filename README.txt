Connect your Deepal or Changan car to your smart home. See the battery, range and charging at a glance, and use the dashboard widget and Flows to lock the car, start the climate or turn on the seat and steering wheel heating. Built and tested on a Deepal S05.

This is an unofficial app. It is not made or endorsed by Changan or Deepal, and you use it at your own risk. Create a dedicated Changan account for Homey, because logging in can sign that account out of the My Changan app, and set the remote-control PIN in the My Changan app before you log in here.
